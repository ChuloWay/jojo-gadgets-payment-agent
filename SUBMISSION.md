# TSG Labs Build Exercise submission draft

## Role preference

Forward-deployed Engineering and Technical Product Management.

## Business context

Jojo Gadgets is a real gadget business in Computer Village, Ikeja, Lagos. The owner is Uchenna Joseph Etokwudo. Discovery notes confirm the shop's transfer-and-receipt process, manual bank-app verification, experience with fake alerts, manual stock book, suitability of a checkout link, and default three-month warranty. No incident rate or financial loss amount was supplied, and the notes are paraphrased rather than verbatim quotations.

All customer identities, phone numbers, inventory rows, prices, Paystack transactions, and detailed address data in the build are synthetic. The three-month default warranty comes from discovery, while its item-specific coverage and remedies remain unconfirmed.

## Problem statement draft

Jojo Gadgets sells laptops, consoles, and phones from Computer Village, Ikeja. Customers pay by bank transfer and send a receipt to the shop's WhatsApp Business account, but owner Uchenna Joseph Etokwudo still opens his bank app to confirm the credit because the business has received fake payment alerts; during service downtime, he records the customer's number and follows up later. This Chord teammate replaces that manual matching step for agent-generated payments by quoting controlled test inventory, temporarily allocating the selected item, creating a Paystack test checkout with a unique reference, and confirming payment only by verifying that reference. Delivery logistics, trade-ins, installments, negotiation, refunds, temporary virtual accounts, live credentials, and real customer data are deliberately outside the demonstration.

The monetary value of attempted fraud and the weekly volume of manual checks remain unmeasured, so the submission does not invent either figure.

## Synthetic demo identity and policies

- **Business:** Jojo Gadgets
- **Owner:** Uchenna Joseph Etokwudo
- **Demo location:** Computer Village, Ikeja, Lagos; no street address or real pickup details are used
- **Warranty:** Three months by default; it may vary with the acquisition deal for a specific device
- **Returns:** No policy has been supplied; all requests go to owner review
- **Owner review:** Faults, replacements, cancellations, refunds, delivery, trade-ins, bulk orders, and payment disputes

The confirmed details can be used in the test conversation. Item-specific warranty coverage and unconfirmed return terms must not be presented as established business policy.

## Remaining discovery questions

1. About how many WhatsApp payment checks happen in a typical week?
2. How many minutes does a normal check take, and how long can it take during service downtime?
3. Has a fake alert caused a financial loss, or has manual checking caught every attempt?
4. Who besides Uchenna can update the stock book or confirm payment?
5. What are the exact warranty exclusions, return terms, pickup process, and delivery policy?

Record the answers faithfully. One concrete quote and one measured fact are more useful than invented detail.
